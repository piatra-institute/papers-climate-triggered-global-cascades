# Brief

## Question

Could anthropogenic climate change contribute to one billion excess deaths in
some rolling 365-day period? What model would be required to distinguish an
unsupported catastrophe story from a calibrated estimate of a climate-triggered
global cascade?

## Claim

No current model establishes a defensible probability for a billion-death year.
Direct hazard studies do not span the food, trade, energy, finance, governance,
conflict, displacement, disease, and relief pathways required by the claim. A
credible test needs a stochastic hybrid causal model on a temporal multilayer
network, explicit buffers and recovery clocks, behavioral feedback, competing
mortality hazards, rare-event methods, and paired factual-counterfactual climate
ensembles. The model must be calibrated on crises and non-collapses, and it must
be able to reject the catastrophic pathway.

## Kind

Synthesis and methods paper. It specifies a formal model but does not ship the
global simulation, which would require a multi-institution data and validation
program. Accordingly, `has_simulation: false` and `claims_target: none`.
