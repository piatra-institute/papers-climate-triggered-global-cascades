## Structured bibliography (2026-09-23)

The inline reference list was migrated to references.yaml (CSL) and in-text citations to Pandoc [@id] syntax. 15 entries were resolved through doi.org content negotiation (14 from their DOIs, one matched in Crossref) and checked for year and title; Cline (2021) was assigned the Princeton University Press DOI of the revised edition; the IPC manual, the UN World Population Prospects summary and the WHO fact sheet were entered by hand from their URLs. Organisation ids were renamed for legibility (ipcc2022, nasem2016, undesa2024, ipc2021, who2024).
Corrections found in the migration: the IPC Technical Manual Version 3.1 was issued in 2021 (confirmed by a 2026 Food Policy article citing IPC Global Partners 2021a), not 2026; the WHO malnutrition fact sheet currently online is dated 1 March 2024, not 2023; the DOI records give the first author of Arnscheidt et al. as Constantin W. Arnscheidt and of Linkov et al. as Igor Linkov (the legacy list had initials J. and M.). Source claims in claims.yaml were checked against abstracts from Crossref, OpenAlex, PubMed or the publisher, the open-access full text of Arnscheidt et al., the AR6 WGII Summary for Policymakers, the UN DESA key-messages document and the WHO page. The IPC famine threshold of 2 deaths per 10,000 per day was not re-verified because ipcinfo.org refused automated access.
